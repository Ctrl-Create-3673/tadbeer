TADBEER FOR MAC - HOW TO OPEN IT THE FIRST TIME
================================================

Tadbeer is not sold through Apple, so your Mac asks you to approve it once.
This is normal. It takes about a minute and you only do it one time.

1. Double-click Tadbeer-mac-arm64.dmg.
2. Drag the Tadbeer icon onto the Applications folder.
3. Open Applications and double-click Tadbeer.
4. A message says Apple could not verify "Tadbeer".
   Click "Done". Do NOT click "Move to Trash".
5. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
6. Scroll down to the Security section.
   - "Allow applications from" must be set to "App Store & Known Developers".
   - Just below it you will see: "Tadbeer" was blocked to protect your Mac.
   Click "Open Anyway" and enter your Mac password or use Touch ID.
7. Click "Open" if your Mac asks once more.

Tadbeer now opens normally every time, along with its small widget.

If it says "Tadbeer is damaged and can't be opened":
Open Terminal and run this, then open Tadbeer again:

    xattr -cr /Applications/Tadbeer.app

Prefer no steps at all? Open https://tadbeer-edb8b.web.app in Safari or Chrome
and add it to your Dock or Home Screen. The website version needs no approval.

For Macs with Apple chips (M1 and newer).
