Tadbeer privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

What Tadbeer stores

Your tasks, events, notes, reminders and settings are stored only in your own browser or device (IndexedDB). We do not run a server and cannot see them.

Google sign-in

If you choose "Sign in with Google", Tadbeer asks for your name, email address, profile picture and permission to create and edit events on your own Google Calendar. It uses this only to add your upcoming Tadbeer items to your calendar so Google can send you reminders. The access token stays in your browser's memory for about an hour and is never saved or sent to us. Tadbeer changes only the events it created and does not read your other events. You can sign out in the app at any time and remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Tadbeer's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Feedback form

The Feedback page embeds a form hosted by Tally (tally.so). What you type there is sent to Tally and the Tadbeer owner under Tally's privacy policy. It is optional.

No tracking

Tadbeer has no analytics, advertising or tracking. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs.

Deleting your data

Use Settings, then "Clear all data" to erase everything stored on your device, or clear the site's data in your browser.

Contact

Use the feedback form to reach the Tadbeer owner.
